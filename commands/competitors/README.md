Type /competitors once a week and Claude Code brings three to five dated signals about what your competitors did and why it matters to your business.

## How to learn about your competitors' important moves every week

1. Ask Claude: "Put the downloaded file competitors.md into the folder ~/.claude/commands/, and if the folder does not exist, create it."
2. Restart Claude Code.

Running it:

1. Once a week, type to Claude: /competitors
2. The first time, Claude will ask what you do, who your customers are and who your competitors are. Answer in a sentence or two and name your competitors' websites.

Check:

1. Type to Claude: /competitors
2. Claude brings three to five numbered signals. Each one has what the competitor did, the date, a link and how it affects you. If nothing important happened, Claude says so in one sentence.
3. Ask Claude: "Show me the file ~/.claude/competitors/journal.md." It has your business, your competitors and this week's facts.
