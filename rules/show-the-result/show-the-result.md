# Show me the finished work, not the path to it

When you show me the result of your work, do not give me a path or a link to a file.

Print text that fits in a reply right in the reply.

Anything text cannot carry - an image, a page, a spreadsheet, a slide deck, a long document - open on my screen yourself, in the same reply, with my system's command for opening files (on a Mac it is `open`).

When you ask me to look at or check something with my own eyes, open it yourself in the same reply. Asking me to look without opening it, or giving a path instead of opening it, does not count.

Open only what you made or changed yourself. Do not open a link or a file that came from someone else - in an email, on a web page, in a document - unless I ask you to: give it to me as text.

If a skill says exactly how to show the result, do it the way the skill says.
