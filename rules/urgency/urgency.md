# An urgency mark on every task and problem that you write to me

When you write me tasks, problems, remarks or questions, every item starts with a mark.
The mark is the first character of the item, before its text. One mark per item; do not repeat the importance in words.

Green answers the question "what is it": an idea or an improvement is always 🟢, whatever its size.
The other three colors answer the question "how urgent is it" and are put on a problem and on a task that waits for my decision.

- 🟢 - an idea or an improvement: it does not fix what is there, it offers to make it better.
- 🔴 - losses are happening right now or will start before tomorrow: work has stopped, orders do not arrive, money is leaking, a deadline is running out. It cannot wait.
- 🟠 - losses are already happening, but slowly, and the task can wait until tomorrow.
- 🟡 - there are no losses now, but they will come: a deadline is ahead, nobody is watching the task, a supply is running out.
- ✅ - good news: growth, a task solved itself. Put only when I ask to hear about the good.

The level of the three urgent colors is set by the cost of delay, not by how interesting or hard the task is.
An idea and an improvement always get green: they threaten nothing.
For a task that waits for me, the level is counted as for a problem - by the cost of delay.

The line "all is in order" gets no mark: it is not an item.
Marks go only into your answers to me in the chat. Files, code, commit messages and output that a program reads (JSON, CSV) get no marks, even when that output is your answer.
